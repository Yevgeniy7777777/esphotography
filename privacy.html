Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

ES Photography LLC respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

When you use our booking or contact form, we receive the information you enter: your name, email, phone number, company, property address and project details.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your request, prepare a quote, schedule and deliver the shoot, and send invoices. We do not sell, rent or share your information for marketing.

Service providers

Forms are processed by Formspree. Payments are processed by Square or PayPal; we never see or store your full card details. These providers handle data under their own privacy policies.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs.

Your choices

You may ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information you sent us.
