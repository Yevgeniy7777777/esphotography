Shoot Preparation

A well-prepared property photographs better and saves time on site. Please review this checklist before your shoot.

Exterior

  • Move vehicles away from the facade and main entrance, especially service vehicles.
  • Hide dumpsters and trash bins; sweep the entrance and walkways.
  • Exterior and sign lighting on only for twilight shoots.

Interior

  • Turn on all lights and replace burned-out bulbs.
  • Open blinds evenly.
  • Turn off TVs and monitors.
  • Clear desks and counters of papers, boxes, personal items and loose cables.
  • Push chairs in neatly.
  • Clean windows, glass doors and floors.

Access

  • Keys or access cards ready for the scheduled time.
  • An on-site contact name and phone number.
  • Tenants and building security informed about the shoot and drone flight.

Before the shoot

  • Send a list of must-have shots and any areas that should not be photographed.

Please complete visible repairs before the shoot. We do not digitally remove damage or defects, as this would misrepresent the condition of the property.
